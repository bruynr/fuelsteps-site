# What's new

Source of https://fuelsteps.com/releases/ (English only). Newest first; one `## <date> · <label>` section per entry, label `App <version>` (the Connect IQ Store version, text as in the app's CHANGELOG.md and the store's "What's new") or `Website`. ## 2026-10-29 · App 1.0.1

- Your schedule now works like a workout: after a saved or discarded run, or when you leave the activity before the start, it is off again. Fixes reminders that could fire on your next run.

## 2026-10-23 · App 1.0.0

First release in the Connect IQ Store.

- Settings in Garmin Connect: one text field per schedule (paste the import text from the plan builder), five schedules on one page
- Up to 5 fuel schedules of your own, by distance or time, filled in Garmin Connect and chosen on the watch
- Steps with their own distance/time, repeat, text (Gel, Dextro, Gel CAF, …), carbs and caffeine
- Vibration, tone (if your watch has one) and a full-screen alert, 60 s / 150 m before each moment (adjustable)
- Countdown to the next alert, with a ring on a full screen; works on half and quarter screens too
- Pick a schedule per run: after a saved or discarded run FuelSteps is off again, like a navigation route
- The last moment stays on the field: amber "Gel · now" for a while after the alert, then "Gel · km 20", so a missed alert is never a mystery
- Planned carbs, g/h, fuel moments and caffeine written to the activity (FIT), visible in Garmin Connect
- English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Italian
